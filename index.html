                  Methods and sources

                  Forecast

                  • NWS Salt Lake City gridded forecast (2.5 km) for the cells holding Painter Spring (SLC 38,112) and Marjum Pass (SLC 41,114). Chance of rain is per forecast period; the running total adds the forecast amounts (QPF) from now on, which NWS issues about 3 days ahead, so a blank tail means no amount yet, not no rain.

                  Rain history

                  • Radar at the two points: NCEP Stage IV hourly (radar corrected with gauges) for windows up to 7 days, and NOAA MRMS daily totals for longer windows, both read from the IEM IEMRE point service. Radar here looks down from far away (Salt Lake and Cedar City), so light rain and low showers are often missed.
                  • Gauges: Tule Valley SCAN (NRCS, hourly, from AWDB), Tule Valley RAWS (BLM, hourly cumulative), King Canyon (UDOT, 10 min) and Snake Valley (DRI) for context, from MesoWest. Increments are summed; negative steps count as 0; a jump over 10 mm in 10 min is left out unless another gauge also recorded rain within 30 min. No data is shown as no data, not 0.
                  • Soil moisture: SCAN sensors at 2, 4, 8, 20 and 40 in. A jump at 2 in that fades over a day or two is rain that soaked in.
